CapyTap: Market is a cozy pixel-art idle clicker about a capybara with a big dream. Capy arrives in a tiny town with a single coin and a corner shop. Tap, hire helpers and watch it grow into a six-floor shopping mall. Then jump through time and build it all again, bigger and stranger.

What's inside

  • Tap, hire, grow: 13 animal helpers, 6 shop levels, and a mall you build floor by floor.
  • Travel through time: 9 eras, from Renaissance streets and Disco nights to Neon, Space and a lantern-lit Japan with a paper dragon. Every era has its own town, music and customers.
  • 8 secret bonus games with prizes, and 60+ skins to dress Capy up.
  • Seasons, events, a quest diary and the travelling Fortune Circus.
  • Free and friendly: plays in your browser on phone and PC, saves automatically, no ads, English and Ukrainian.

Found a bug or have an idea for the next era? Leave a comment or use the red mailbox in the game. If Capy made you smile, a rating on this page helps a lot. Thank you!

Тапай, наймай помічників і дивись, як вона виростає в шестиповерховий торговий центр. А потім стрибай крізь час і будуй усе заново, більше й чудернацькіше.
